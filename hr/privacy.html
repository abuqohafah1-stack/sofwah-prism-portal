1. Who we are

NARA HR (The People Management Platform) is the staff app used by Sofwah Arabiata (M) Sdn. Bhd. (brand: Sofwah Arabic Grill), Kedah, Malaysia ("Sofwah", "we"). It is used only by Sofwah employees for attendance, schedules, leave, claims, payslips and announcements. Sofwah is the data user under the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA).

2. Data we collect

DataWhenWhy
Name, staff ID, branch, position, section and work emailFrom your employment recordTo identify you and show your own schedule and records
Email and passwordWhen you create your accountTo sign in. Passwords are stored only as a salted hash, never in readable form
Precise location (GPS)Only at the moment you clock in or clock outTo confirm you are at your branch. The app does not track you in the background

We do not use advertising, advertising IDs, analytics trackers or data brokers, and we do not sell or rent your data.

3. Who can see it

  • You can see your own data in the app.
  • Your branch manager sees your attendance, schedule and requests for your branch.
  • HR and Sofwah management see records needed for HR, payroll and compliance. Payslips are visible only to you and authorised HR/payroll staff.
  • Service providers that host the app for Sofwah: Google (Google Workspace / Apps Script, where records are stored), Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (notifications) and GitHub Pages (hosts the app files; no personal data). They process data only on Sofwah's instructions.
  • Government bodies (for example KWSP, PERKESO, LHDN) when the law requires it.

4. How long we keep it

  • Attendance, payroll and employment records: at least 6 years, as required by the Employment Act 1955 and related laws.
  • Your sign-in account (password, PIN, devices, calendar link): until you delete your account or leave Sofwah.
  • Clock-in selfies: kept with the attendance record they verify.

5. Your choices and rights

  • Access and correction: you may ask HR to see or correct your personal data (PDPA sections 30 and 34).
  • Permissions: you can turn location, camera and notifications off at any time in your phone Settings. Clock in needs location and camera at that moment.
  • Delete your account: in the app, open Me › Delete account. This erases your password, PIN, calendar link and notification devices and signs you out on every phone. Employment records that the law requires us to keep stay with Sofwah. To ask for other data to be erased, contact HR.

6. Security

All connections use HTTPS. Passwords and PINs are hashed with a secret pepper. Access is limited by role and branch, sessions expire, and accounts lock after repeated wrong attempts.

7. Children

NARA HR is for Sofwah employees only and is not directed to children.

8. Contact

Sofwah Arabiata (M) Sdn. Bhd. — HR Department
Contact HR through your branch manager or HQ, or via sofwaharabicgrill.com.

3. Siapa boleh melihatnya

  • Anda boleh melihat data anda sendiri dalam app.
  • Pengurus cawangan melihat kehadiran, jadual dan permohonan bagi cawangannya.
  • HR dan pengurusan Sofwah melihat rekod yang perlu untuk HR, gaji dan pematuhan. Slip gaji hanya dilihat oleh anda dan staf HR/gaji yang diberi kuasa.
  • Penyedia perkhidmatan yang mengehos app untuk Sofwah: Google (Google Workspace / Apps Script, tempat rekod disimpan), Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (notifikasi) dan GitHub Pages (mengehos fail app; tiada data peribadi). Mereka memproses data atas arahan Sofwah sahaja.
  • Badan kerajaan (contohnya KWSP, PERKESO, LHDN) apabila diwajibkan undang-undang.

4. Tempoh simpanan

  • Rekod kehadiran, gaji dan pekerjaan: sekurang-kurangnya 6 tahun, seperti diwajibkan Akta Kerja 1955 dan undang-undang berkaitan.
  • Akaun log masuk (kata laluan, PIN, peranti, pautan kalendar): sehingga anda memadam akaun atau berhenti kerja.
  • Selfie clock in: disimpan bersama rekod kehadiran yang disahkannya.

5. Pilihan dan hak anda

  • Akses dan pembetulan: anda boleh meminta HR untuk melihat atau membetulkan data peribadi anda (PDPA seksyen 30 dan 34).
  • Kebenaran: anda boleh mematikan lokasi, kamera dan notifikasi bila-bila masa dalam Tetapan telefon. Clock in memerlukan lokasi dan kamera pada ketika itu.
  • Padam akaun: dalam app, buka Me › Delete account. Ini memadam kata laluan, PIN, pautan kalendar dan peranti notifikasi, dan log keluar daripada semua telefon. Rekod pekerjaan yang wajib disimpan mengikut undang-undang kekal dengan Sofwah. Untuk meminta data lain dipadam, hubungi HR.

6. Keselamatan

Semua sambungan menggunakan HTTPS. Kata laluan dan PIN di-hash dengan rahsia tambahan. Akses dihadkan mengikut peranan dan cawangan, sesi tamat tempoh, dan akaun dikunci selepas cubaan salah berulang.
