Bantuan

For Sofwah Arabic Grill staff · Untuk staf Sofwah Arabic Grill

NARA HR — The People Management Platform — is the staff app used by Sofwah Arabiata (M) Sdn. Bhd. Clock in and out, see your shifts, apply for leave, submit claims, read payslips and HR announcements.
NARA HR ialah app staf yang digunakan oleh Sofwah: clock in/out, jadual syif, cuti, tuntutan, slip gaji dan pengumuman HR.

Getting started · Mula guna

Create your account · Buat akaun

Open the app, tap Create account, enter the work email registered with HR, type the 6-digit code we email you, then choose a password (8+ characters with letters and numbers).
Tekan Create account, masukkan e-mel yang didaftarkan dengan HR, taip kod 6 digit dari e-mel, kemudian pilih kata laluan.

Forgot password · Lupa kata laluan

Tap Forgot password? on the sign-in screen and follow the same email code steps.
Tekan Forgot password? dan ikut langkah kod e-mel yang sama.

“Email not registered” · “E-mel tidak berdaftar”

Ask HR or your branch manager to add or correct your email in your staff record.
Minta HR atau pengurus cawangan menambah atau membetulkan e-mel anda dalam rekod staf.

Clock in · Clock in

On Today, press and hold the round button until the ring fills, release, take a selfie, and the app records the time and your location at that moment.
Di Today, tekan dan tahan butang bulat sehingga cincin penuh, lepaskan, ambil selfie. App merekod masa dan lokasi anda ketika itu.

Location or camera blocked · Lokasi atau kamera disekat

iPhone: Settings › NARA HR › Location: While Using the App, Camera: on.
Android: Settings › Apps › NARA HR › Permissions › Location: Allow only while using the app, Camera: Allow.
Clock in di luar kawasan cawangan tetap direkod dan pengurus dimaklumkan.

Leave, claims, payslips · Cuti, tuntutan, slip gaji

  • Requests tab: apply for leave (attach MC photo for sick leave) or submit a claim with a receipt photo.
  • Me tab: payslips (optionally protected by a 6-digit PIN), announcements, calendar sync.
  • Tab Requests: mohon cuti atau tuntutan. Tab Me: slip gaji, pengumuman, kalendar.

Delete your account · Padam akaun

In the app: Me › Delete account, enter your password. Your sign-in, PIN, calendar link and notification devices are erased. Employment records required by law stay with Sofwah. See the Privacy Policy.
Can’t open the app? Ask HR (through your branch manager or HQ) to delete your NARA HR account for you — no need to reinstall the app.
Dalam app: Me › Delete account. Tidak dapat buka app? Minta HR (melalui pengurus cawangan atau HQ) padamkan akaun NARA HR anda. Rekod pekerjaan yang diwajibkan undang-undang kekal dengan Sofwah.
